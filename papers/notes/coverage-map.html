The finding. Nine algorithms are missing from your DP4_manual.pdf. Four of the nine are dynamics and two are tube amps — precisely the two disciplines your paper folder has nothing on. The manual gap and the literature gap are the same gap.

Which means the two papers that close them — Giannoulis for dynamics, Yeh's thesis for tubes — settle the theory for five of the nine algorithms whose manual pages you don't have. The remaining four (8 Voice Chorus, EQ-Chorus-DDL, 3.3 sec Delay 2U, Dual Delay) are already covered by Effect Design Part 2, which you hold.

And a cheap shot at the scan itself: Dattorro hosts a German edition of the manual, dp4_Deutschland.pdf. Same plates, separate typesetting run. If the omission is a defect in the English export rather than the source, those pages are in the German file. One download.
