Abstract
We know that the cone of Euclidean distance matrices does not intersect the cone of positive semideﬁnite
matrices except at the origin in the subspace of symmetric matrices. Even so, the two cones can be related
by an equality.

3. Conclusion
Although its roots lie in the algebra of Schoenberg, we derived our main result (2) via estab-
lished projection theory given by Moreau and by Deutsch. Equality (2) is a recipe for constructing
the EDM cone whole from large Euclidean bodies: the positive semideﬁnite cone, orthogonal
complement of the geometric center subspace, and symmetric hollow subspace.
